# WP-200 Heat Pump – Operation

## Heating curve
The heating curve defines the flow temperature depending on the outdoor temperature.
For underfloor heating a slope between 0.3 and 0.6 is recommended; for radiators between 0.8 and 1.2.

## Hot water
The default hot water temperature is 48 °C. Once per week the legionella program heats the tank to 60 °C.

## Quiet mode
Quiet mode reduces the compressor speed between 22:00 and 6:00. Sound power drops from 55 dB(A) to 49 dB(A).
