# WP-200 Heat Pump – Error codes

## E12 – High pressure
The refrigerant high pressure switch has tripped. Check whether the heating circuit is open and the circulation pump runs.
If E12 appears more than three times in 24 hours, contact service.

## E31 – Outdoor sensor
The outdoor temperature sensor is disconnected or defective. The unit continues in emergency mode with a fixed flow temperature of 35 °C.

## E45 – Defrost failure
Defrosting did not finish within 15 minutes. Remove snow or leaves from the evaporator and restart the unit.
