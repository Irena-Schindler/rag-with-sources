# WP-200 Heat Pump – Installation

## Location
The outdoor unit needs at least 50 cm clearance on the air intake side and 150 cm in front of the fan.
Do not install it in a corner or under a closed carport roof, because cold air recirculates and efficiency drops.

## Electrical connection
The WP-200 requires a dedicated 16 A circuit breaker (type C) and a residual current device of type A.
Only a qualified electrician may connect the unit.

## Commissioning
After installation, flush the heating circuit and set the system pressure to 1.5 bar.
The installer must enter the heating curve during commissioning; the factory default is slope 0.8.
